Language science & language technology

From fieldwork to production systems.

I am engaged in two kinds of research: language science and language technology. I love to help extend our collective understanding of both how human interaction works and what computational systems can usefully do with speech and language.

Language science Language technology

01

Language science

I've mostly been interested in language as a complex system shaped by the social and cognitive constraints of human interaction and learning. We can model aspects of speech and language as mathematical or computational systems, while holding lightly to these abstractions and tightly to the data details, knowing the abstractions are incomplete. The full publication list is on Google Scholar.

02

Language technology

Since my first computational linguistics class more than 25 years ago, computational systems have become much more useful for processing speech and language, and yet the path from an experimental prototype to a reliable production system still depends on a combination of user-centered design, pragmatic engineering, and rigorous evaluation.
