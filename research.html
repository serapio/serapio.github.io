Research & writing

Language, examined closely.

I work on lexical tone, word prosody, discourse structure, and the evaluation of language technology. The archive below keeps the academic work online; the writing links are where the newer questions end up.

01

Threads

The subjects I keep returning to, whether the data arrives as a fieldwork recording or a production eval set.

Phonetics & phonology

Tone, stress, and word prosody

Acoustic and phonological work on tone languages, especially Ixpantepec Nieves Mixtec, Jinhua Wu, and Choguita Rarámuri. Description first, then measurement.

Discourse

Structure and segmentation

Hierarchical discourse segmentation and the problem of evaluating it when section breaks differ in importance and location. Still a useful test case for any metric.

Evaluation

Multilingual language technology

What happens to ASR, NLU, and LLM systems when they leave English: failure analysis, annotation quality, and evaluation that pays attention to the language rather than the average.

Writing

Essays, notes, and marginalia

Shorter pieces on language, evaluation, dogs, and the occasional semicolon. The blog is where unfinished thoughts are allowed to remain unfinished.

02

Selected archive

Publications, talks, and materials from the academic side of the filing cabinet.

  • Dissertation
    Mixtec

    Ixpantepec Nieves Mixtec word prosody

    A phonological description and acoustic study of stress and tone in the Mixtec spoken by the diaspora community in San Diego, with talks at Meso-American Linguistics, SSILA, and the Conference on Indigenous Languages of Latin America.

  • NAACL 2010
    MA thesis

    Evaluating hierarchical discourse segmentation

    A metric for hierarchical segmentation that accounts for differing section importance and imprecise break locations, tested on encyclopedia articles and expository speech.

  • Fieldwork
    Gitonga

    Inhambane Gitonga

    Work on final pitch lowering, penultimate vowel lengthening, and blocked multiple exponence in an issue of SDLP compiling grammatical descriptions of Gitonga.

  • Acoustic study
    Jinhua Wu

    A contour tone chain shift in Jinhua Wu

    A variationist acoustic study of generational change in a complex tone sandhi system in central Zhejiang Province.

  • Documentation
    Rarámuri

    Choguita Rarámuri Documentation Project

    Assistance with prosodic studies and data management, integrating FLEx lexical data, Praat acoustic analysis, and ELAN annotations.
