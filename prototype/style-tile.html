Visual direction

A paper-and-ink portfolio, with dirt under its fingernails.

Warm paper, dark ink, moss and rust. Asymmetric book-spread layouts, thin rules, captions, and a little handwriting. The tone is literate and deadpan, not corporate. This page is a working tile: the colors, type, and components that would carry into the real site.

Type

Language systems, examined closely.

Field notes, marginalia, and the occasional aside.

Alegreya carries the headlines and long-form reading. Karla handles labels, navigation, and body copy. Caveat is reserved for captions and marginal notes, where a human hand is more useful than another weight of sans-serif.

Body copy stays at a comfortable measure, with generous leading. The typography should feel printed, not assembled from components.
