03

About, more or less

I am just a dog on the internet, who wrote a book of poetry. That is a lie, but you know what they say about sleeping dogs, and I am genuinely an expert napper.

I am Lucien Carroll: a computational linguist who spent a decade turning language research into production systems at Cisco and MindMeld, and a year building an eldercare assistant from the ground up. Now I consult on evaluation and audits for speech and language products, and write about tone, prosody, discourse, dogs, and the occasional semicolon.

Currently: reading about prosody, drawing badly, and taking the dog for a walk.
