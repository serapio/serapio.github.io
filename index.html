03

About, more or less

I have spent my career asking whether the system actually works. It turns out that is still a controversial position.

I am Lucien Carroll: a computational linguist and data scientist with a research background in speech and low-resource languages, and practical expertise in data development and system evaluation. I spent a decade turning language research into production systems at MindMeld and Cisco, then a year building an eldercare assistant from the ground up. Now I consult on evaluation and audits for language-based products, with a focus on healthcare, and write about tone, prosody, discourse, and the occasional semicolon.

Currently: reading about prosody, drawing badly, and taking the long way home.
